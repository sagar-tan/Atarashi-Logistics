3. FULL DESCRIPTION (Maximum 4,000 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Atarashi is India's next-generation intra-city on-demand logistics and parcel delivery platform. Designed for modern individuals, small businesses, and enterprises, Atarashi delivers your packages across town quickly, safely, and transparently.

Whether you need to send urgent documents across the city, transport retail goods, move household essentials, or dispatch commercial freight, Atarashi connects you with verified delivery partners in minutes.

KEY FEATURES:

🛵 Multi-Vehicle Fleet for Every Load
• 2-Wheeler Courier: Fast, cost-effective delivery for parcels up to 20 kg (documents, parcels, clothes, groceries, electronics).
• 3-Wheeler Auto / Cargo: Ideal for medium consignments up to 300 kg (boxes, home appliances, business inventories).
• Trucks & Freight (Tata Ace, Pickup 8ft): Heavy commercial freight and furniture shifting up to 1,000 kg.
• House & Office Shifting: Dedicated micro-relocation support with careful handling.

⚡ Real-Time Transparent Pricing
• No hidden fees or surprise surge charges.
• Upfront distance-based fare calculation in paise with clear invoice breakdowns.
• Multiple payment options: UPI, Wallets, Net Banking, Credit/Debit cards (via Razorpay), or Cash on Delivery.

📍 Live GPS Courier Tracking
• Real-time map tracking of your assigned delivery courier from pickup to drop-off.
• Live ETA updates and instant courier contact via masked in-app calling.

🔒 Safe & Verified Delivery
• Secure 4-digit OTP verification at pickup and delivery to ensure zero package mix-ups.
• Complete digital Proof of Delivery (POD) with photo confirmation and digital signature.
• 100% verified, background-checked delivery partners.

🏢 Atarashi for Business (B2B Logistics)
• Automated GST-compliant invoicing with input tax credit support.
• Multi-stop routing to deliver to multiple clients in a single trip.
• Dedicated enterprise dashboard for high-volume order dispatch and tracking.

🇮🇳 Fast, Secure Login
• Frictionless 10-digit mobile number login with instant SMS OTP.
• Secured with hardware-backed Android KeyStore AES-256 encrypted authentication.

Download Atarashi today and experience effortless intra-city parcel delivery!
