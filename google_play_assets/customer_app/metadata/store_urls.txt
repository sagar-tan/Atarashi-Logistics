# Atarashi Customer App - Mandatory Web Endpoints for Google Play Console

1. Privacy Policy URL:
https://atarashi.in/privacy
(Must be publicly accessible via HTTPS and detail location, phone number, and payment processing).

2. Account Deletion URL (Mandatory per Google Play Policy):
https://atarashi.in/account-deletion
(Web form allowing users to submit their phone/email to delete account & order data without reinstalling the app).

3. Support Email (Publicly visible on Store Listing):
support@atarashi.in

4. Developer Website:
https://atarashi.in

5. Data Safety Form Answers:
- Location (Approximate & Precise): Collected for pickup/drop-off geocoding and live delivery tracking.
- Personal Info (Name, Phone number, Email): Collected for account management and OTP authentication.
- Financial Info (Payment history): Collected for digital receipts and billing records.
- Security Practices: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.3). User can request data deletion.
