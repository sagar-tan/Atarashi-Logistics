Partner with Atarashi and earn top income delivering parcels, goods, and freight in your city!

Whether you own a Bike, an Auto/3-Wheeler, a Tata Ace, or a 4-Wheeler Pickup Truck, the Atarashi Driver app gives you flexible earning opportunities with complete freedom, transparent payouts, and steady daily delivery requests.

WHY DRIVE WITH ATARASHI?

💰 Instant Daily Payouts & Highest Earnings
• Get paid directly into your linked bank account via UPI with minimal withdrawal wait times.
• Highly competitive per-kilometer and minimum base rates calculated down to the paise.
• Zero arbitrary deductions. Transparent trip fare calculation before you accept any delivery.

⚡ Flexible Working Hours
• Be your own boss: Work part-time or full-time on your schedule.
• Simple One-Tap Online/Offline toggle — switch on the app when you are ready to earn, and turn it off when you are done.

📍 Smart Route Radar & Minimum Dry Runs
• Advanced proximity dispatch algorithm assigns orders closest to your current location.
• Built-in turn-by-turn map navigation with optimized polyline routes to save fuel and time.
• Multi-stop support so you can pick up multiple consignments in the same geographic cluster.

🛵 All Vehicle Categories Supported
• 2-Wheeler (Bike / Scooter): Fast intra-city courier, food, documents, and lightweight parcel deliveries.
• 3-Wheeler Cargo / Auto: Commercial boxes, wholesale consignments, and consumer electronics.
• Trucks & Mini-Trucks (Tata Ace, Bolero Pickup, 8ft): Furniture, industrial freight, and shifting services.

🔒 Simple & Safe Delivery Process
• In-app caller mask ensures your personal contact number remains private.
• Digital Proof of Delivery (POD) via photo capture and secure OTP confirmation.
• Dedicated 24/7 driver partner support team to assist you during active deliveries.

EASY 3-STEP ONBOARDING:
1. Download the Atarashi Driver app and register with your mobile number.
2. Upload basic documents (Driving License, Vehicle RC, Aadhaar/PAN, Bank/UPI details).
3. Complete quick profile verification and start accepting orders to earn immediately!
